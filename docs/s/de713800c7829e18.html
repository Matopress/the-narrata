Ghana announces first-ever hydrocarbon discovery in carbonate reserve

Africanews · 12 Nov 2026

Ghana's President John Dramani Mahama on Wednesday announced the country's first-ever hydrocarbon discovery in an offshore carbonate reserve. The well lies some 150 kilometers off Ghana's coast in water more than 2000 meters deep.
