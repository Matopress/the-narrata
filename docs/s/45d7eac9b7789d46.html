Joshua Baraka Becomes First African Artist Featured On Johnnie Walker Bottle

Nile Post · 25 Nov 2026

Ugandan musician Joshua Baraka has become the first African artist to feature on a Johnnie Walker bottle, with the global whisky brand unveiling a limited edition ahead of his November 28 B’raka Land concert.
