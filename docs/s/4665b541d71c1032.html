Belgium: Prince Harry and Meghan visit EU Parliament for child safety event

Africanews · 10 Nov 2026

Prince Harry and his wife Meghan visited the European Parliament in Brussels, on Tuesday 10 November, to discuss the dangers children face online. The couple met families affected by online abuse before Harry addressed the gathering.
