ANC vows "soul searching" after election losses but says Ramaphosa will stay put

Africanews · 12 Nov 2026

Days after a drubbing at the ballot box, the leaders of South Africa's ruling African National Congress met to discuss the way forward. In last week's local elections, the party's vote share dropped by 15 points to just 31 percent.
