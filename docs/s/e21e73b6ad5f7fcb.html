Mauritanian anti-slavery activist Biram Dah Abeid resigns from parliament

Africanews · 10 Nov 2026

Prominent Mauritanian human rights activist and anti-slavery advocate Biram Dah Abeid announced his resignation from parliament Monday citing "widespread corruption" in the west African country, where he is an opposition lawmaker.
