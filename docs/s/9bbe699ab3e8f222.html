Ntungamo Proposes Shs77bn Budget, Allocates Only Shs3.3bn to Development

Nile Post · 12 Nov 2026

The proposed 2027/2028 budget allocates Shs55 billion to wages and Shs18 billion to non-wage expenditure, leaving limited funding for development amid staffing shortages and service delivery gaps.
