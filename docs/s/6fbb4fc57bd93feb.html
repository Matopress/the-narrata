Haiti gang suppression force says it has killed multiple gang members

Africanews · 24 Nov 2026

The force said at least six suspected gang members were killed and five rifles seized during clashes on the Marassa Bridge, which connects Tabarre to Croix-des-Bouquets, a stronghold of the powerful 400 Mawozo gang.
