Spain: 69 candles lit at Toledo Cathedral for train crash victims

Africanews · 10 Nov 2026

Spain's King Felipe VI and Queen Letizia attended a state funeral at Toledo Cathedral on Tuesday 10 November for the 69 people killed in a devastating high-speed train collision near Urda on 28 October.
